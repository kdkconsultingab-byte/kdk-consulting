Who we are

KDK Consulting AB is a Swedish company based in Gothenburg, Sweden. We develop software products and mobile applications, and provide technology consulting. KDK Consulting AB is the data controller for personal data described in this policy.

Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to communicate with you and operate this website. This may include your name, email address and the information you choose to include when you contact us.

Contact form and email data

Our website currently provides contact by email and does not include a contact form. If you email us, we use your contact details and message only to respond to your enquiry and manage any resulting business relationship. If we add a contact form in the future, this policy will be updated before it is used.

Analytics

We do not currently use website analytics or advertising tracking on this website. If we introduce analytics in the future, we will update this policy with the provider, purpose and relevant choices.

Cookies

We do not currently use non-essential cookies on this website. Our hosting provider may process limited technical information needed to deliver the site securely and reliably. If we introduce non-essential cookies, we will ask for consent where required.

Future apps and product data

As KDK releases software products and mobile applications, each product may have its own privacy information tailored to the data it uses. Product-specific privacy information will be provided before or when a product collects personal data. This website policy does not describe data practices for products that have not yet been released.

Data retention

We keep personal data only for as long as needed to respond to an enquiry, provide services, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes or enforce agreements. We then delete or anonymise it when it is no longer needed.

Third-party services

We use service providers where necessary to host and operate this website and to provide email communication. These providers process data only as needed to provide their services and under appropriate safeguards. We do not sell personal data.

Your rights under GDPR

If the GDPR applies to you, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of processing of your personal data. You may also have the right to object to certain processing and to receive a portable copy of data you have provided. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time.

You also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. In Sweden, this is the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).
